═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SVG TECHNICAL RULES (violations get stripped by my importer)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

- Root must be: <svg xmlns="http://www.w3.org/2000/svg" viewBox="0 0 W H"> … </svg>. xmlns and viewBox are both mandatory.
- One line, valid JSON string: every internal double quote escaped as \" and no raw newlines. No markdown fences around it.
- Dark strokes on a white/transparent background: stroke="#111" stroke-width="2" fill="none". Presentation attributes only — no <style>, no CSS classes.
- BANNED: <script>, <foreignObject>, <image>, <iframe>, any on* handler, any external URL, @import, external fonts.
- Fonts: only font-family="serif" or "sans-serif", font-size 11–16. Unicode is fine inside <text> (Ω, θ, μ, °, →, ²).
- Keep each figure under ~12 KB. Schematic, not illustration.
- Primitives: line, path, circle, rect, polyline, polygon, ellipse, text, g, marker, defs.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HOW TO DRAW EACH KIND OF FIGURE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CIRCUITS — resistor as a zig-zag path or open rectangle, cell as long/short parallel lines, capacitor as two parallel plates, inductor as loops, ammeter/voltmeter as a circle labelled A / V, switch as a hinged line. Label every component with its value from the question (4 Ω, 10 V, 2 μF). Show current direction with a filled triangle arrowhead. Series vs parallel must be visibly correct.

GRAPHS — both axes with arrowheads, axis labels with units exactly as printed, origin marked O, plus every tick, intercept, asymptote or turning point the answer depends on. The curve's SHAPE must be right (linear / parabolic / exponential decay / saturating / sinusoidal are all distinguishable at a glance).

RAY OPTICS — dashed principal axis, lens as a double-arrow ellipse or mirror as an arc with hatching behind it, F and 2F marked, object as an upright arrow, at least two construction rays with arrowheads, image drawn on the correct side with the correct orientation.

MECHANICS / FBD — blocks as rectangles, pulleys as circles, strings as lines, inclines with the angle arc labelled (θ = 30°), every force as a labelled arrow (mg, N, T, f). Mark given masses and lengths.

GEOMETRY / VECTORS — label all vertices, arc and label every angle, tick equal sides, dash the construction lines, arrowhead and label every vector.

MOLECULAR STRUCTURES — skeletal line-angle formulae: vertices are carbons, heteroatoms written as <text> (O, N, Cl, OH, NH\u2082, COOH). Double bond = two parallel lines, triple = three. Benzene = hexagon with three alternating inner double bonds (be consistent). Stereochemistry: bold wedge as a filled narrow-to-wide <polygon>, dashed bond via stroke-dasharray, and label R/S or cis/trans when the question turns on it. Reaction schemes: a <marker> arrow with reagents above and conditions below.

APPARATUS (calorimeter, U-tube, manometer, Young's double slit, potentiometer, Wheatstone bridge) — outline the vessel/setup, light fill or hatching for liquids, label every dimension and every named part.

ACCURACY — the figure must be solvable-equivalent to the original: every quantity, angle, label and connection the solution uses must appear, and nothing may contradict the PDF. Geometric fidelity beats prettiness. Never invent a value that is not in the PDF; if a label is unreadable, omit that label rather than guessing.
