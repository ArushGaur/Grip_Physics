Getting started

Do we have to re-type our existing question papers?

No. Upload the PDFs, Word files or scans you already have. Our importer extracts questions, options and solutions into a review pool, where your faculty approve or fix them before they enter the bank.

How long does onboarding take?

Most institutes are running their first generated paper within a week: two days for setup and branding, two to three for bank migration, and one training session for faculty.

Migrating a large legacy archive runs in parallel and never blocks day-to-day use.

Who sets it up — us or you?

We do. Branding, subjects, chapters, batches and the first question import are handled by your onboarding manager, then handed over with a training session for your faculty.

Can we try it before committing?

Yes — the live institute panel demo is open with sample data and nothing to install. For a hands-on session with your own paper, book a demo.

Papers & question bank

Does it handle maths, chemistry and diagrams?

Yes. Equations are preserved as real formulas rather than flattened images, and figures are cropped and attached to the question they belong to.

Can we still print papers instead of going fully online?

Yes. Print-ready DOCX and PDF output with your letterhead is a first-class feature, not an afterthought. Many institutes use Vyorra only for paper generation at first.

Can we control which chapters and difficulty appear in a paper?

Yes. Set a blueprint — marks per chapter and per difficulty band — and the generator respects it instead of picking at random. You can also swap any individual question before exporting. See how paper generation works →

What if the same question gets imported twice?

Duplicates are detected and flagged in the review pool rather than silently added, so the same item does not appear twice in one paper.

Online tests

How is cheating handled?

Attempts are bound to a device and roll number, tab-switching is logged, and repeat attempts are blocked. Flags go to a teacher review queue — the system never punishes a student automatically.

What do students need to take a test?

A phone or laptop and their roll number. They log in with an OTP, see assigned tests with deadlines and marking scheme, and attempt them in a locked test window.

When do results come out?

Objective papers are scored the moment the window closes, with instant scorecards and solutions for students and chapter-wise analytics for faculty.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes. The institute app is available for Android on Google Play with the same dashboard, question library, attendance and results as the web panel. The student portal also installs as an app on any phone. See the app →

Privacy & access

Is our question bank visible to other institutes?

Never. Every institute is isolated with its own data scope, its own passcodes and its own branding. Content you upload stays yours.

Can we limit what individual teachers can do?

Yes. Sensitive staff actions sit behind a passcode layer inside the panel, and a faculty activity view records who added questions and who set papers.

Can we export our data if we leave?

Yes. Papers and keys export as DOCX and PDF, and student, result and attendance records export as sheets. Your archive is never locked in.

Billing

What does Vyorra cost?

₹15,000 per year for every 100 students, covering both panels, the Android app, onboarding, question migration and faculty training. See the full slab table →

What happens if our student count grows mid-year?

You move to the next slab and pay only the difference for the remaining months. Nothing stops working while you upgrade.

Are papers, tests or imports billed separately?

No. Usage is unlimited on every slab — you are billed only on student count, with no setup fee on annual plans.
