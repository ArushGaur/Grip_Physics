Frequently asked questions

The questions institute owners and academic heads ask us most often, answered plainly.

Do we have to re-type our existing question papers?

No. Upload the PDFs, Word files or scans you already have. Our importer extracts questions, options and solutions into a review pool, where your faculty approve or fix them before they enter the bank.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes. The institute app is available for Android on Google Play, with the same dashboard, question library, attendance and results as the web panel. The student portal also installs as an app on any phone.

Is our question bank visible to other institutes?

Never. Every institute is isolated with its own data scope, its own passcodes and its own branding. Content you upload stays yours.

How is cheating handled?

Attempts are bound to a device and roll number, tab-switching is logged, and repeat attempts are blocked. Flags go to a teacher review queue — the system never punishes a student automatically.

What happens if our student count grows mid-year?

You move to the next slab and pay only the difference for the remaining months. Nothing stops working while you upgrade.

How long does onboarding take?

Most institutes are running their first generated paper within a week. Migration of a large legacy archive runs in parallel and does not block day-to-day use.

Does it handle maths, chemistry and diagrams?

Yes. Equations are preserved as real formulas rather than flattened images, and figures are cropped and attached to the question they belong to.

Can we still print papers instead of going fully online?

Yes. Print-ready DOCX and PDF output with your letterhead is a first-class feature, not an afterthought. Many institutes use Vyorra only for paper generation at first.
