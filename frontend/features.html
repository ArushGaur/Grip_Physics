The workflow

Four moves, start to result sheet

Nothing is re-typed, nothing is re-formatted, and nothing lives in a folder only one teacher can find.

  1. 1

    Import

    Drop in PDFs, DOCX files, scans or phone photos of handwritten sets. The importer reads every question on the sheet.

  2. 2

    Review

    Faculty approve or correct each item in a review pool before it enters the bank, so nothing unchecked reaches a student.

  3. 3

    Generate

    Pick a blueprint and get a print-ready paper with letterhead, instructions, answer key and solutions — or publish it as an online test.

  4. 4

    Analyse

    Scores, chapter-wise weakness and attendance roll up automatically, per student and per batch, the same evening.

Question bank

Build the bank once. Use it for a decade.

Most institutes already own years of question papers scattered across PDFs, Word files, printouts and handwritten sets. Vyorra pulls questions, options, answers, solutions and diagrams out of those files and files them under the right subject, chapter and topic.

  • Import from anything — PDF, DOCX, scanned images and photographed handwritten sets.
  • Maths and diagrams survive — equations stay real formulas, figures are cropped and attached to their question.
  • Tagged automatically — subject, chapter, topic, difficulty and question type, all editable.
  • Duplicate detection — the same question imported twice is flagged, not silently duplicated.
Paper generation

Print-ready papers, not rough drafts

Pick a chapter mix, a difficulty spread and a total mark count, and Vyorra assembles the paper. Output is a clean DOCX or PDF carrying your letterhead, instructions block and marking scheme — ready for the photocopier without a single reformat.

  • Blueprint control — set marks per chapter and per difficulty band; the generator respects it instead of picking at random.
  • Multiple sets — Set A / B / C with shuffled order and matched difficulty to cut copying in a crowded hall.
  • Keys included — every paper ships with its answer key and solution sheet, generated at the same moment.
Analytics

Know the weak chapter before the next class

Results roll up into per-student, per-batch and per-chapter views as soon as a test window closes. You see which topic a batch is losing marks on while there is still time to re-teach it — not after the term test.

  • Student-level score history across every test taken.
  • Chapter-wise weakness reports for a whole batch.
  • Question-level breakdowns — spot the item everyone got wrong.
  • Faculty activity view: who added questions, who set papers.
Attendance

One register, visible to everyone who needs it

Mark a batch from the web panel or the Android app in seconds. Students see their own record day by day, and you can export a register for parent communication without rebuilding it in a spreadsheet.

  • Batch-wise marking with a running monthly percentage.
  • Per-student attendance profile, shared with the student portal.
  • Exportable registers for parent meetings and audits.
