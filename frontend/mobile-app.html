The Institute App, in Your Pocket

Vyorra ships a dedicated Android app for institutes. Directors and faculty can check the dashboard, open the question library, mark attendance and read results between classes — same data as the web panel, built for one thumb.

What you get on your phone

  • Full dashboard: total students, question bank size, tests conducted and latest results — the same numbers your web panel shows.
  • Question library: browse, search and review questions by subject, chapter and topic, right from your phone.
  • Attendance: mark a batch's attendance on the go and check any student's attendance history.
  • Test results: see live submissions, student scores and question-level breakdowns as soon as a test window closes.
  • Bottom-tab navigation: Home, Library, Students, Attend, Results — one tap to switch.

For students too

The student portal installs as an app on any phone (Android and iOS), so students do not need the Play Store to take a test. They log in with roll number and OTP, see assigned tests, attempt them in a locked test window, and track scores, streaks and attendance day by day.

Same data, everywhere

A question added on the web is instantly visible on the app. Results from an app-taken test appear on the web panel. No syncing, no delay.

No extra licence cost

The Android app is included with every annual plan. You don't pay more for your faculty to use it on their phones.

Works on any Android phone

Lightweight and tested on devices from budget phones to flagships. No special hardware required.

Available on Google Play

The Vyorra institute app is available on Google Play. During onboarding, your institute gets set up on the app alongside the web panel — same branding, same passcodes, same data scope.
