How slabs work

Counted in hundreds, never in surprises

Pricing is counted in slabs of 100 students. A 240-student institute pays for three slabs. If your roll grows mid-year, you move to the next slab and pay only the difference for the remaining months — nothing stops working while you upgrade.

Billing questions

Before you ask

Is there a setup or implementation fee?

No. On annual plans, onboarding, question migration and faculty training are part of the price.

What happens if our student count grows mid-year?

You move to the next slab and pay only the difference for the remaining months. Nothing stops working while you upgrade.

Are we billed per paper or per test?

Never. Papers, online tests and question imports are unlimited on every slab. You are billed only on student count.

Do we pay extra for the Android app?

No. The institute app is included, and the student portal installs as an app on any phone. See what the app does →

Can we run multiple branches on one account?

Yes. Multi-branch groups are quoted together and get consolidated reporting across centres while each branch keeps its own data scope.

Tell us your student count and we will send a written quote the same day — along with a 30-minute walkthrough on your own question papers.
