What the price includes

  • Institute panel and student portal — both, not sold separately.
  • The Android app for your institute at no extra licence cost.
  • Question migration — we import your existing papers during onboarding.
  • Faculty training so your teachers can set papers without calling support.
  • Unlimited papers and tests — you are never metered on usage.
  • Support Mon–Sat, 9am–8pm IST by phone and email.

How the slabs work

Pricing is counted in slabs of 100 students. A 240-student institute pays for three slabs. If your student count grows mid-year, you move to the next slab and pay only the difference for the remaining months — nothing stops working while you upgrade.
