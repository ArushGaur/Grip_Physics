How slabs work

Counted in hundreds, never in surprises

Pricing is counted in slabs of 100 students. A 240-student institute pays for three slabs. If your roll grows mid-year, you move to the next slab and pay only the difference for the remaining months.

Billing questions

Before you ask

Is there a setup or implementation fee?

No. Onboarding, question migration and faculty training are part of the annual price.

What happens if our student count grows mid-year?

You move to the next slab and pay only the difference for the remaining months.

Are we billed per paper or per test?

Never. Papers, online tests and question imports are unlimited on every slab.

Do we pay extra for the Android app?

Yes — the Android institute app is a one-time ₹15,000, not per device or per year. See what the app does →

Can we run multiple branches on one account?

Yes. Multi-branch groups are quoted together and get consolidated reporting across centres.
